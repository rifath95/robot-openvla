Seen in training: red, blue, green, yellow. Other colors are unseen. Three and four cubes are also outside the training scenes.

Choose sampling or an upward move, then greedy prediction assesses progress for at least 12 actions. Upward recovery holds horizontal position and gripper setting, with a height ceiling of 55 cm above the world origin (30 cm above the table). Detection uses only robot feedback, never cube positions or contacts. The action limit includes recovery moves.

Move up pauses model actions, discards a pending prediction, and raises the arm up to 10 cm using the same height ceiling. It works with any recovery setting. Afterward the arm holds; press Play or Act once to resume. Play continues from the current pose. Stop holds the pose and discards pending predictions. Reset restores the initial arm and layout. Instructions are sent exactly as typed; scoring follows the selected target.
